# 🚀 Welcome to ForgeOps

ForgeOps is an **AI-Native Software Engineering Platform** built to transform the way engineering teams understand, build, maintain and evolve enterprise software.

Instead of acting as another coding assistant, ForgeOps introduces a new generation of **AI Software Engineers** capable of collaborating with developers through natural conversations, understanding complete repositories, navigating architectures, reviewing source code, analyzing documentation and continuously improving software systems.

The platform combines **Artificial Intelligence**, **Enterprise Architecture**, **Knowledge Management**, **Event-Driven Engineering**, **Cloud Native Infrastructure** and **Modern DevOps** into a unified engineering ecosystem.
