# ForgeOps Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and community members, are committed to making participation in the ForgeOps community a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

ForgeOps is an international open source project. We believe that great software is built by diverse communities where people collaborate openly, respectfully, and professionally.

Regardless of experience level, background, nationality, culture, language, gender, gender identity, age, disability, appearance, ethnicity, religion, or any other personal characteristic, every participant deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

We expect all members of the ForgeOps community to contribute to an environment where ideas can be discussed constructively and disagreements can be resolved professionally.

---

# 🌍 Our Mission

ForgeOps is more than a software project.

Our mission is to build an open, collaborative ecosystem that advances AI-native engineering, Platform Engineering, DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Software Architecture, and Intelligent Automation.

We recognize that collaboration is essential to achieving this vision.

Our community should be a place where:

* Knowledge is shared openly.
* Questions are welcomed.
* Constructive feedback is encouraged.
* Diverse perspectives are valued.
* Contributors help each other grow.
* Technical discussions remain respectful.
* Everyone feels safe participating.

# 💬 Communication Guidelines

Healthy communication is fundamental to an open source community.

When communicating:

* Stay on topic.
* Use clear language.
* Be respectful.
* Avoid sarcasm directed at individuals.
* Avoid personal attacks.
* Keep technical discussions evidence-based.
* Welcome different viewpoints.

Remember that written communication can easily be misunderstood.

When in doubt, choose clarity and kindness.

---

# 🌐 International Community

ForgeOps is a global project.

Participants come from different countries, cultures, and native languages.

Please:

* Be patient with language differences.
* Avoid slang that may confuse others.
* Write documentation in English.
* Use inclusive and accessible language.
* Respect cultural differences.

Our goal is to make participation accessible to contributors worldwide.

---

# 🏗 Technical Discussions

Technical disagreements are natural and often beneficial.

When discussing architecture or implementation:

* Support opinions with technical reasoning.
* Consider alternative solutions.
* Respect architectural decisions.
* Accept consensus once decisions have been documented.
* Continue discussions professionally even when opinions differ.

Healthy debate improves software.

Personal conflict does not.

# ForgeOps Code of Conduct (Part 02)

# ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

To maintain a healthy and welcoming community, certain behaviors are not acceptable under any circumstances.

The following examples are illustrative and are not intended to be exhaustive.

## Harassment

Harassment of any kind is prohibited.

Examples include:

* Offensive comments.
* Personal insults.
* Intimidation.
* Threats.
* Deliberate humiliation.
* Repeated unwanted contact.
* Stalking behavior.
* Deliberate exclusion of individuals.

---

## Discrimination

ForgeOps does not tolerate discrimination based on any personal characteristic.

Every participant should be treated fairly and respectfully.

---

## Hate Speech

Content that promotes hatred, violence, or hostility toward individuals or groups is strictly prohibited.

This includes:

* Derogatory language.
* Dehumanizing statements.
* Calls for violence.
* Symbols or imagery intended to intimidate or promote hate.

---

## Personal Attacks

Critique ideas—not people.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Insults.
* Mocking contributors.
* Public humiliation.
* Name-calling.
* Questioning someone's worth or intelligence.
* Aggressive or hostile communication.

Technical disagreement should never become personal conflict.

---

## Harassment Through Technology

Online harassment is treated the same as in-person harassment.

This includes behavior through:

* GitHub Issues.
* Pull Requests.
* Discussions.
* Documentation comments.
* Chat platforms.
* Email.
* Social media when interacting as part of the ForgeOps community.

---

## Trolling

Deliberately provoking conflict or disrupting productive discussions is unacceptable.

Examples include:

* Repeated inflammatory comments.
* Intentional misinformation.
* Persistent off-topic arguments.
* Disruptive behavior intended to derail conversations.

---

## Spam

Do not use project communication channels for:

* Advertising.
* Unsolicited promotions.
* Irrelevant commercial content.
* Repeated duplicate messages.

---

## Publishing Private Information

Never publish another person's private information without explicit permission.

Examples include:

* Home addresses.
* Phone numbers.
* Personal email addresses.
* Government identification.
* Private conversations.
* Confidential company information.

---

## Misrepresentation

Do not intentionally misrepresent:

* Your identity.
* Your affiliation.
* Your contributions.
* Project decisions.
* Security issues.
* Official project positions.

Transparency builds trust.

# ForgeOps Code of Conduct (Part 03)

---

# 🚨 Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it as soon as possible.

Prompt reporting helps maintain a healthy, respectful, and welcoming community for everyone.

Reports should be made **privately** whenever possible.

Public discussions about individual incidents should be avoided while an investigation is in progress.

---

# 📬 How to Report

Reports may be submitted through one of the following channels:

* Private email to the project maintainers.
* GitHub's private security reporting feature (when appropriate).
* The contact information provided in the project's `SUPPORT.md` or `SECURITY.md`.

When reporting an incident, please include as much relevant information as possible, such as:

* Description of the incident.
* Date and time.
* Communication channel.
* Links to relevant discussions.
* Screenshots (if applicable).
* Any additional context that may help the investigation.

Providing detailed information helps the maintainers understand the situation and respond appropriately.

---

# 🔍 Investigation Process

Every report will be reviewed carefully.

The general process includes:

1. Acknowledgement of the report.
2. Preliminary review.
3. Collection of relevant information.
4. Discussion among project maintainers.
5. Decision regarding appropriate actions.
6. Communication of the outcome to the reporting party when possible.

Investigations should be conducted impartially and with respect for everyone involved.

---

# 🔒 Confidentiality

Privacy is extremely important.

Project maintainers will make reasonable efforts to:

* Protect the identity of the reporting individual.
* Protect sensitive information.
* Limit access to incident details.
* Share information only with those directly involved in resolving the issue.

Absolute confidentiality cannot always be guaranteed, particularly when legal obligations or platform requirements apply, but every reasonable effort will be made to respect privacy.

---

# ⚖ Possible Consequences

Responses to violations will depend on the severity, frequency, and context of the behavior.

Possible actions include:

## Informal Reminder

For minor issues, a private reminder or clarification may be sufficient.

---

## Formal Warning

A contributor may receive a formal warning describing:

* The problematic behavior.
* Why it violates the Code of Conduct.
* Expectations for future participation.

---

## Temporary Restrictions

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may temporarily restrict participation, including:

* Commenting.
* Opening Issues.
* Participating in Discussions.
* Submitting Pull Requests.

---

## Removal from the Community

Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from ForgeOps community spaces.

This may include permanent restrictions from participating in project activities.

---

# 🤝 Good Faith

Reports should always be submitted in good faith.

Knowingly submitting false, misleading, or malicious reports may itself be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.

The goal of the reporting process is to protect the community—not to create conflict.

---

# 🌍 Fairness

Maintainers are committed to handling reports fairly.

Whenever possible, decisions should be based on:

* Available evidence.
* Project policies.
* Context.
* Consistency with previous decisions.

Every participant should be treated with respect throughout the process.
